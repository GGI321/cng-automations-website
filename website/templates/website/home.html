Frequently asked questions

Is business automation right for you?

What is AI automation?

AI automation combines AI capabilities with software workflows to help interpret information, prepare responses, classify requests, and trigger defined business actions. Appropriate rules and human review remain important.

What is the difference between CRM automation and email automation?

CRM automation organizes customer and lead records, assignments, tasks, and follow-ups. Email automation handles workflows such as categorizing messages, drafting replies, sending approved notifications, and scheduling follow-ups. The two can work together.

Can automation help a small business?

Yes. Small businesses can start with one repetitive process, such as lead capture, appointment reminders, customer enquiries, or moving information between applications. The right starting point depends on the time and cost of the existing process.

Will AI send replies without human approval?

It can be configured either way. Routine, low-risk replies may be automated when the information and rules are clear. Sensitive, uncertain, or high-impact requests can be sent to a person for approval.

How do we know which workflow to automate first?

Start with a task that happens frequently, takes significant staff time, causes delays, or produces avoidable errors. We can discuss the current process and assess whether automation is appropriate.
