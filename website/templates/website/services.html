Industry 01

Real Estate & Property Management

Property enquiries can arrive through website forms, email, social media and WhatsApp. Without a consistent process, agents may spend valuable time sorting messages instead of speaking with serious prospects.

Example: from enquiry to viewing

  1. 01 A prospective tenant or buyer submits an enquiry.
  2. 02 Automation records their details and property preferences in the CRM.
  3. 03 An AI assistant can help answer approved property questions and collect missing information.
  4. 04 The agent receives the enquiry, follow-up task or viewing request that needs attention.

What this can improve

Less manual lead entry, clearer follow-up records, fewer enquiries lost in scattered inboxes and more time for agents to focus on clients and viewings.

Property availability, pricing and viewing confirmation should use verified business records and rules.

Industry 02

Hospitals, Clinics & Healthcare

Reception teams may need to answer routine calls, respond to appointment enquiries, send reminders and update booking records while attending to people at the clinic.

Example: an AI voice agent for appointments

  1. 01 A patient calls to ask about clinic hours or request an appointment.
  2. 02 A configured voice agent answers approved routine questions and collects the necessary details.
  3. 03 The workflow checks the connected booking rules before offering available appointment options.
  4. 04 Appointment requests, reminders and exceptions are routed to the appropriate staff member.

What this can improve

Less repetitive call handling, more organized appointment requests and fewer manual reminder tasks for reception staff.

Clinical decisions, urgent symptoms and sensitive patient concerns must be escalated to qualified staff. Healthcare workflows need appropriate privacy controls.

Industry 03

Sales Teams & Business Development

When a business receives many enquiries, manually reviewing every lead and deciding who should follow up can take time away from conversations with potential customers.

Example: AI lead qualification and CRM routing

  1. 01 A lead arrives through a form, email or connected messaging channel.
  2. 02 AI analyzes the enquiry against criteria defined by the business.
  3. 03 The workflow records the lead and routes it for sales follow-up, further questions or human review.
  4. 04 Team members receive relevant context and next steps instead of starting from scratch.

What this can improve

Less time spent sorting leads, more consistent CRM records, clearer ownership and more organized follow-up.

Industry 04

Customer Service & Operations

Repeated customer emails, unanswered messages, order updates and internal notifications can create a constant stream of small tasks for your team.

Example: email automation and agentic replies

  1. 01 An incoming email or message triggers a workflow.
  2. 02 AI identifies the request and retrieves relevant information from approved business sources.
  3. 03 The workflow drafts or sends an appropriate reply according to the business's approval rules.
  4. 04 Complex, sensitive or uncertain requests are flagged for a human instead of being guessed at.

What this can improve

Less repetitive inbox work, more consistent responses, better task visibility and fewer manual handoffs between tools.

Common questions

Before we automate anything

Do I need to replace my existing business software? +

Not necessarily. Where suitable integrations are available, we can connect your existing CRM, email, forms, databases and other business tools. The best approach depends on your current systems and requirements.

Can AI reply to customers automatically? +

Yes, when the channel, integration and workflow support it. Replies can use approved information and business rules. You can also require human approval for sensitive, uncertain or high-impact messages.

Can you automate our CRM and email together? +

A connected workflow can, for example, capture an enquiry, create or update a CRM record, notify the responsible team member and trigger an appropriate email follow-up. The exact workflow depends on your software and access.

How do we know whether automation will save us money? +

We start by identifying how often a task occurs, how much staff time it takes, the cost of the current process and the likely implementation and running costs. That gives us a more realistic basis for deciding whether automation is worthwhile.

Is automation suitable for a small business? +

It can be. A small, well-defined workflow—such as organizing leads, sending confirmations or reducing repetitive customer replies—may be a better starting point than a large, complex system.
